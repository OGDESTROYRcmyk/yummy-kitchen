🍜 Egg Soy-Sauce Noodles

A quick noodle bowl with egg and a tasty soy-sauce and ketchup twist.

⏱️ ~10 min💰 ~QAR 5
🥣 Ingredients
  • 1 serving noodles
  • 1 egg
  • 1–2 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp ketchup
  • Optional: a little oil or butter
👩‍🍳 How to make it
  1. Cook the noodles according to the packet instructions.
  2. Drain most of the water.
  3. Cook the egg in a pan until safely cooked through.
  4. Add the noodles, soy sauce and ketchup and stir.
  5. Top with the egg and enjoy!
💡 Price is an estimate and depends on the shop and brand.

🥪 Chicken Salami Toastie

Crispy buttery bread with chicken salami and ketchup.

⏱️ ~10 min💰 ~QAR 6
🥣 Ingredients
  • 2 slices bread
  • Chicken salami
  • A little butter
  • Ketchup
  • Optional: 1 egg
👩‍🍳 How to make it
  1. Spread a little butter on the outside of the bread.
  2. Put chicken salami between the slices.
  3. Heat a frying pan over medium heat.
  4. Cook the sandwich on both sides until golden and crisp.
  5. Add ketchup. If using an egg, cook it separately until safely cooked through.
🔥 Ask an adult for help with the hot pan if you need it.

🍳 Egg & Bread

A super simple breakfast or snack using basic ingredients.

⏱️ ~5–10 min💰 ~QAR 3
🥣 Ingredients
  • 1 egg
  • 1–2 slices bread
  • Optional: a little butter or oil
  • Optional: ketchup
👩‍🍳 How to make it
  1. Heat a pan over medium heat with a little butter or oil.
  2. Cook the egg until safely cooked through.
  3. Toast the bread.
  4. Put the egg on the toast and add ketchup if you like.
💡 Keep it simple or add your favourite toppings!
